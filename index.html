{{ site.tagline }}

I am an economist and PhD Fellow at HEC Lausanne. My research covers firm productivity, international trade and global value chains. At the Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH), I work with the CompNet team on a cross-country Micro Data Infrastructure that gives researchers and policymakers comparable firm-level data across Europe.

Before my PhD, I was a research fellow at VU Amsterdam and worked in the WTO's Economic Research and Statistics Division. I also write on trade, technology and geopolitics for a general audience.
