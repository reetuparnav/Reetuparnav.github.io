For most of the twentieth century, oil was power. In this piece I argue that advanced semiconductors now play that role. I look at why the concentration of leading-edge manufacturing in Taiwan makes the supply chain a flashpoint, how the United States, China and Europe are building capacity at home, and why India needs a self-reliant chip ecosystem of its own.
