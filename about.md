I am a PhD Fellow in Economics at **HEC Lausanne**, University of Lausanne, and a consultant with **CompNet** at the **Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH)**, a member of the Leibniz Association. My research covers firm productivity, international trade and global value chains, using firm-level data across European countries.

Before starting my PhD, I was a research fellow at **Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam**, interned in the **WTO**'s Economic Research and Statistics Division, and collaborated with the **University of Oxford** and the **World Trade Institute** on an [SNSF-funded project](https://data.snf.ch/grants/grant/200968) on labour standards in global supply chains. In 2023 I delivered a statement to the UN Human Rights Council on behalf of the NGO OCAPROCE. I am a member of the [Global Advisory Council](https://org.foreignpolicyindia.in/about-us/global-advisory-council) of the Indian Foreign Policy Project.

I welcome enquiries about research collaborations in economics, finance, data analysis and policy.
