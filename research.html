Research projects

  1. CompNet Micro Data Infrastructure (MDI) · Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH), 2023–present
    I help develop the cross-country Micro Data Infrastructure of CompNet, the Competitiveness Research Network, which gives researchers and policymakers comparable firm-level data across European countries. I lead collaborations with government economists on research using the MDI, work with national statistical institutes, and train researchers on its tools. From 2024 to 2025 I worked on the infrastructure as a research fellow with Eric Bartelsman at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
  2. Labor Standards Compliance and Buyer-Supplier Linkages in Global Supply Chains · University of Oxford and World Trade Institute, University of Bern, 2023
    Research collaborator on this SNSF-funded project led by Matthew Amengual and Damian Raess, which studies the governance systems that monitor and improve labour standards in global supply chains.
    SNSF project page
  3. COVID-19 and social behaviour · Aalto University, 2020
    Research assistant to Juuso Välimäki and Pauli Murto, combining economic and epidemiological models to study how the pandemic changed social behaviour.

Talks and posters

  1. The Digitalized Economy (poster, with Daniele Aglio) · 14th CompNet Annual Conference, Vilnius, 25–26 September 2025
    Presented in the poster session on applications of the Micro Data Infrastructure. Using harmonised firm-level data for France and the Netherlands, we build a digital adoption score from six ICT components and relate it to exporting and productivity. Digitalization is associated with a higher probability of exporting in both countries and with higher labour productivity in France, and online sales are positively associated with TFP.
    Poster (PDF) · Conference programme
