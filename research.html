Research projects

  1. 2023–present · Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH)

    CompNet Micro Data Infrastructure (MDI)

    I help develop the cross-country Micro Data Infrastructure of CompNet, the Competitiveness Research Network, which gives researchers and policymakers comparable firm-level data across European countries. I lead collaborations with government economists on research using the MDI, work with national statistical institutes, and train researchers on its tools. From 2024 to 2025 I worked on the infrastructure as a research fellow with Eric Bartelsman at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

  2. 2023 · University of Oxford and World Trade Institute, University of Bern

    Labor Standards Compliance and Buyer-Supplier Linkages in Global Supply Chains

    Research collaborator on this SNSF-funded project led by Matthew Amengual and Damian Raess, which studies the governance systems that monitor and improve labour standards in global supply chains.

    SNSF project page

  3. 2020 · Aalto University

    COVID-19 and social behaviour

    Research assistant to Juuso Välimäki and Pauli Murto, combining economic and epidemiological models to study how the pandemic changed social behaviour.

Talks and posters

  1. 25–26 September 2025 · 14th CompNet Annual Conference, Vilnius

    The Digitalized Economy

    Poster with Daniele Aglio, presented in the session on applications of the Micro Data Infrastructure. Using harmonised firm-level data for France and the Netherlands, we build a digital adoption score from six ICT components and relate it to exporting and productivity. Digitalization is associated with a higher probability of exporting in both countries and with higher labour productivity in France, and online sales are positively associated with TFP.
